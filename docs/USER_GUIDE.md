# User Guide

1. **Create an account** - Register with your name, email and a password.
2. **Take the skill assessment** - Select every skill you have, then pick
   one as your primary skill.
3. **Review your recommendations** - Your dashboard shows business ideas
   matched to your primary skill.
4. **Open a business idea** - See the investment range, expected income,
   difficulty and required skills.
5. **Learn what you need** - Each business idea links to courses, PDF
   guides, certifications and video tutorials.
6. **Follow the roadmap** - Check off each step as you complete it; your
   progress is saved to your account.
7. **Connect with a mentor** - Browse mentors who specialize in your
   skill area and reach out directly.

Your skill selection and progress are also cached in your browser, so the
site keeps working even if you're briefly offline - it syncs back to your
account the next time you save a step while signed in.
